Leonida Nights v2 — a GTA-style cover art maker: your photo + caption become a multi-panel poster with bold lettering and a strong sunset/neon grade, exported as PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080. v2 is "cover-first": the cover leads (screenshot 1 is the poster full-frame), the renderer is more vivid (Vice Blaze grade, dense 7-panel Poster Grid and 9-panel Nine Grid, bigger stroked title), the editor is slimmer, the title drags to a new spot, and dropping a photo onto a panel swaps it in. One click exports all three post sizes. Eight panel layouts, six gradient-map grades, halftone title block, tilted snapshot strip, badge ribbon, per-panel drag/zoom, undo/redo. Static, offline, no build step. Fonts Anton & Pacifico under the SIL Open Font License (see app/fonts/OFL.txt).

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
